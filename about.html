About EV Revival

I built EV Revival from real hands-on experience with Vestel EV chargers and the setup and access challenges owners can face.

I kept seeing the same pattern: charger apps and setup journeys were often unclear, too technical, or hard to follow when something went wrong.

So I created EV Revival to explain things in plain English, especially around connectivity, setup, and common charger issues.

This site does not replace manufacturer guidance, installer advice, or specialist diagnostics. My aim is to make everyday steps clearer, reduce frustration, and help owners make better self-service decisions.

EV Revival now supports compatible Innogy chargers alongside Vestel chargers. The existing setup and troubleshooting guides remain specific to Vestel models.
