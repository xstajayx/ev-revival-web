Set up your Innogy eBox with eConfig

Connect your compatible Innogy eBox to EV Revival using the eConfig app and your charger ID.

Before you start

  • Have your charger ID ready. You will use it in the OCPP address below.
  • Keep your charger’s setup details and manufacturer instructions available.

1. Download eConfig

Download the eConfig app from your phone’s app store.

Open the app and follow the on-screen setup instructions for your Innogy eBox.

2. Select a third-party backend

During setup, select 3rd party backend.

3. Update the backend URL

Enter your OCPP address using your charger ID in place of <CBID>:

OCPP address:
wss://ocpp.jworldcreations.com/ws/<CBID>

Replace the entire <CBID> placeholder, including the angle brackets, with your own charger ID. Do not enter the placeholder literally.

Continue through the app’s on-screen instructions to apply the configuration and complete setup.

4. Check the connection

Open EV Revival and check your charger’s connection status. If it does not connect, check that the backend URL matches the address above and includes your correct charger ID.

Go to the AppContact support

This guide is for compatible Innogy eBox chargers. Vestel setup instructions are provided separately.
