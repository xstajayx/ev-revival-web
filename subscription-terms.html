Trial & Subscription Terms

An eligible charger can unlock a 14-day premium trial when activation is confirmed in the app. Eligibility takes previous trial use by charger into account. Check your account for the expiry date.

The trial does not automatically create a paid subscription. To keep premium access, choose the subscription in the app and complete Stripe Checkout. The subscription costs £3.99 a month and renews monthly until cancelled. Check the price and first charge shown by Checkout before confirming.

Manage billing and cancellation in the app’s billing area. Cancelling renewal normally leaves premium available until the paid period ends. A failed payment can restrict paid features. See Refunds & Cancellation for statutory rights and support.
