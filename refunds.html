Refunds & Cancellation

Stop future renewals

Open the app’s billing area and use the Stripe customer portal to cancel. Ordinary cancellation normally stops the next renewal and leaves paid access until the current period ends. Check the cancellation date displayed in the portal.

Statutory cancellation and refunds

UK consumers normally have 14 days from entering a distance service contract to cancel. If you expressly ask us to start providing the service during that period, a proportionate charge may apply where the legal conditions are met. Ordinary cancellation in the portal does not replace your statutory rights or remedies for a service that fails to meet legal requirements.

Contact us

Email jworldcreations@hotmail.com to exercise a statutory cancellation right, request a refund or report a duplicate, accidental or unrecognised charge. Include your account email and payment reference if available. Keep full card numbers, security codes and passwords private. For an unrecognised charge, also contact your payment provider promptly.
