Frequently Asked Questions about Vestel chargers

What is EV Revival?

A self-service platform for compatible Vestel chargers, with free guides/FAQs and optional paid platform access.

Who is EV Revival for?

Customers using supported Vestel charger models in home or small business settings.

Where do I open the Vestel alternative app?

Use evrevival.jworldcreations.com for EV Revival web access. You can also see Vestel alternative app steps for home-screen use.

How long is the free trial?

14 days for new customers.

When does the trial start?

When premium access is first activated in the app.

What happens after the trial ends?

Paid subscription is required to continue premium features.

Is billing recurring?

Yes. The premium plan is billed monthly until cancelled.

Can I cancel anytime?

You can cancel recurring billing through your account area. Access continues until the end of your current paid period.

What if payment fails?

Premium features may be restricted while billing is inactive. Limited account settings/data may be retained for account records and reactivation.

How do I schedule a Vestel charger?

Use your EV Revival account controls where supported and confirm charger time/connectivity first. Start with Help with my Vestel charger and the setup checks in Vestel charger Wi-Fi setup.

Does EV Revival guarantee my charger schedule will work?

No. Scheduling and commands depend on charger firmware, connectivity, and third-party systems.

Why is my Vestel charger showing offline?

Usually Wi-Fi signal quality, router issues, backend settings, or charger firmware behaviour. Use Vestel charger Wi-Fi setup to reconnect and then verify Vestel charger OCPP setup if needed.

Where can I get Vestel charger fault and error help?

Start with Vestel charger fault and error help. For unresolved hardware faults or urgent safety concerns, contact your installer or manufacturer.
