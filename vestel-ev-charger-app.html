Vestel EV Charger App Alternative

EV Revival is a web-based platform for compatible Vestel EV chargers. It is designed for users who want a self-service alternative when existing charger apps are unavailable, unreliable, or no longer connected to a working backend.

Not the official Vestel app

EV Revival is an independent platform. It is not the official Vestel app, not a manufacturer service, and not affiliated with Vestel as an official support channel.

Why users look for an alternative

Many users search for a Vestel app alternative after app issues, backend changes, charger accounts no longer working, or confusion around whether a unit is still connected online. Some also prefer direct browser-based access instead of relying on a mobile store app.

What EV Revival provides

Self-service web platform

Use EV Revival in a browser on desktop or mobile without requiring a separate app-store install.

Setup guidance

Follow practical steps to check charger setup and account access for compatible chargers.

OCPP connection guidance

Get guidance on OCPP connection status and common checks when a charger appears offline.

Charger access features

Where supported by charger model and firmware, EV Revival can provide charger access features for day-to-day use.

Controls where available

Scheduling, RFID management, remote charging actions, and session history may be available depending on setup.

Practical support content

Use linked guides for Wi-Fi setup, faults, and service limits before making hardware or network changes.

Helpful resources: OCPP guide, Wi-Fi guide, Error Help, Pricing, and Terms.

What EV Revival does not do

  • It does not guarantee charger connectivity in every setup.
  • It does not control home Wi-Fi, broadband, or wider internet availability.
  • It does not provide direct one-to-one technical support.
  • It does not replace a qualified EV installer for electrical or safety-critical work.
Important: Charger behavior can vary by hardware revision, firmware version, network conditions, and backend configuration. Always verify charger status directly and use a qualified installer where needed.

Compatible Vestel chargers

EV Revival is intended for compatible Vestel chargers. Before relying on any feature, check your charger model, firmware, and setup status to confirm compatibility.
