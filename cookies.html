Cookies and browser storage

The public EV Revival site uses its own JavaScript for navigation and search. Its service worker caches selected public pages and assets for offline use. The firmware installer uses your browser’s USB permission only when you choose to connect a device.

The separate hosted application uses Firebase Authentication to maintain your signed-in session and browser storage to remember application preferences. Signing out ends the application session. Clearing site storage can remove saved preferences and offline files. Stripe applies its own privacy and cookie policies when you open Checkout or the customer portal.

The website code provides no advertising trackers or optional analytics scripts. Hosting-provider settings operate separately. Contact us via Business Information with privacy questions.
